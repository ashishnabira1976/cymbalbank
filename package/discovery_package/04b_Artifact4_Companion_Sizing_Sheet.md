# Artifact #4b: Whiteboard Companion Notes & Sizing Sheet

The whiteboard capture (Artifact #4) and these companion notes were drafted by Cymbal Bank staff during the discovery workshop at Cymbal Bank headquarters. The sketch shows only the main functional blocks the team wants built. How the blocks connect, which data flows are reads or writes, and how the solution is secured were not decided in the workshop.

The figures below are the customer's working numbers and draft targets as written on the day. They have not been validated.

### Companion Notes & Sizing Reference

| Whiteboard Block | What Cymbal Bank Needs (customer notes) | Working Figures & Draft Targets |
|---|---|---|
| **Customers & Staff** | Serve impacted account holders on the mobile banking app, branch kiosks and web, and internal staff in the COC and contact center. | `20` hubs; demand spikes sharply at a hub when a clearing freeze or systemic outage hits. |
| **Access & Trust Controls** | Only authenticated users may reach the agent. Customer personal and payment data must be protected in prompts, responses and logs. Attempts to manipulate the agent or ask off-corpus questions must be stopped. | Reject unauthenticated requests. Mask card numbers, CVVs, passport IDs, legal names and 6-char Case Locators. `100%` block rate on adversarial override attempts and off-corpus queries. |
| **Compliance Q&A Agent** | Answer questions that need compliance audit data, regulatory policy text, or both. Avoid re-sending the large reference manuals on every turn. | Reference material refreshed every `3,600 seconds` (1 hour); end-to-end p95 latency ≤ 2.5 seconds; unit cost < \$0.15 per resolution (vs. \$18.50 per human call). |
| **Operational Data** | Settlement batch audit logs (`compliance_qa_audit.csv`), customer/analyst RBAC profiles (`analyst_user_mapping.csv`), regulatory metadata (`regulatory_metadata_registry.csv`), hub and remittance dispute records. The agent's identity is read-only: no schema changes, inserts or updates. Queries must be bounded and never read a whole table. | `1,500` settlement batches (`compliance_qa_audit.csv`); `12,500` case records (5 account buckets); `5,000` customer/analyst accounts (4 RBAC tiers: `analyst`, `senior_counsel`, `audit_reviewer`, `system`; 5 loyalty tiers + `fraud_dispute_risk_score`); `20` hubs; `2,200` remittance claims; no `SELECT *`. |
| **Policy & Regulatory Library** | Federal regulatory manuals (`Reg_E_Part_1005_Electronic_Fund_Transfers.pdf`, `Reg_Z_Part_1026_Truth_in_Lending.pdf`, `FFIEC_BSA_AML_Examination_Manual_CDD.pdf`), consumer deposit agreements, outage addendums and partner fee-waiver directories. The archived 2022 manual sits in the same repository as the active one. | Active manual `2024.4` (\$3,800 US domestic deposit dispute cap); archived `2022` manual (\$3,500 cap); agent to answer from the active manual only (`is_active = TRUE`). |
| **Provisional Credit & Refunds** | Regulation E / UK PSR statutory provisional credit, Regulation Z (12 CFR Part 1026) automatic refunds, Cross-Border Remittance SDR liability and ancillary fee refunds. | **Reg E / UK PSR:** `€250` (<1,500 index), `€400` (1,500–3,500 index), `€600` (>3,500 index). **Reg Z Part 1026:** 7-day cash/credit refund for delays `>3h` domestic / `>6h` international; refund of paid ancillary wire/dispute fee (\$75 on CB-503) when verification is delayed `>12h` dom / `>15–30h` intl. **Cross-Border Remittance Protocol:** `1,288 SDR` (~\$1,710) international cap. |
| **Fee Waivers & Case Routing** | Re-route delayed settlements, including onto correspondent partner banks. Issue QR fee-waiver and emergency liquidity vouchers at contracted regional partners only. Apply executive outage waivers to Basic Checking (B-bucket). | Fee-waiver caps: `\$140` NYC, `\$120` CLT, `€150` LON. Daily emergency stipends: `\$65/day` Exec Platinum, `\$40/day` General. `100%` wire/overdraft fee waiver during Outage Event Orion. Partners: JPMorgan Chase, Citibank, HSBC. |
| **CDD & SAR Hold Alerts** | Warn COC dispatch before compliance review panels or batch holds time out under FFIEC BSA/AML (31 CFR Part 1010/1020). | Alert when remaining Compliance Duty Period (CDP) `< 30 minutes`. |
| **Human Review** | High-risk or high-value cases go to a human specialist (`senior_counsel` or `audit_reviewer`). | Escalate when claim `> \$2,500`, medical/hardship emergency, Executive Platinum churn risk, or high `fraud_dispute_risk_score`. |
| **Audit & Monitoring** | Keep a tamper-proof evidentiary record for CFPB/OCC/FCA audits. Security checks must pass before release. The service must survive a regional outage. | `0` secret-scan / static-analysis findings before release; multi-region DR `RTO < 60s`, `RPO = 0`. |
